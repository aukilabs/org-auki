# Meme fitness

Memes are subject to natural selection. Four dimensions govern how well one replicates.

## Dimensions

- **Observability** — How easily is the meme observed? Waving to greet is a fit meme (visible). Curling your toes to greet is not.
- **Fidelity** — How easily can it be copied without mutation? A handshake has high fidelity; a complicated fist-bump chain does not.
- **Self-normalization** — Can it repair itself if the receiver has context? A slurred word is understood from the sentence. Memes self-repair when the receiver's RAM has scaffolding — genes can't.
- **Incentive** — Does propagating it reward the host? Higher social status, belonging, humor — built-in incentives to share. "Saying this makes me cool."

## Applying to content

When writing a piece of content, ask:

- Where will we make this observable? (Which channel, what placement.)
- How easy are we making it for the reader to repeat the core message?
- Is the context scaffolded enough that the reader will fill in gaps correctly?
- Does interacting with the meme reward the reader for spreading it?

A high-fitness meme wins on at least two dimensions — ideally all four.
