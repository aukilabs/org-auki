Shelf compliance detection shipped to production. Navigation accuracy improvements rolling out to pilot locations.

Cactus Spatial AI
01 TL;DR

We shipped shelf compliance detection to production this week — the model now identifies out-of-stock and misplaced items with 94% accuracy, up from 81% in our previous release. Navigation route generation also got significantly faster, which improves the in-store staff experience across our pilot locations.

  • Shelf compliance detection — production release of the updated model. Staff can now view compliance status per shelf section in real time through the Cactus app. Accuracy is up 13 percentage points from the previous version.
  • Navigation route optimisation — rewrote the route generation algorithm to account for real-time congestion signals. Average route time dropped from 4.8 seconds to 2.1 seconds. Staff reach their destinations faster, with fewer unnecessary detours.
  • App performance improvements — reduced cold start time by 40% and cut memory usage on older devices by approximately 30%. Cactus now runs well on devices from 2019 onwards.
03 What's in progress

Multi-floor navigation

Extending navigation to handle multi-floor retail environments. The challenge is transitions — stairs, escalators, and lifts each require different routing logic. Single-floor navigation is mature; multi-floor adds a new class of spatial problem. Currently in testing at one of our pilot locations.

AI shelf recommendations

Building an AI layer that recommends shelf reorganisation based on compliance history and foot traffic patterns. The goal is to shift from reactive compliance checking to proactive layout optimisation. Early results from internal testing are promising.

04 What's next
  • Complete multi-floor navigation testing and roll out to all pilot locations.
  • Ship first version of AI shelf recommendations to a small group of pilot users for feedback.
  • Improve shelf compliance model for high-glare environments — current accuracy drops in direct sunlight conditions.
