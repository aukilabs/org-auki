    Feature extraction falls back to a wider SIFT window when ORB finds fewer than 40 keypoints. Pose accuracy in the Gotu warehouse: 71% → 94%.

    Removed a redundant round-trip from the joining sequence. Domain join latency: 420ms → 180ms.

    Rewrote pose conversion and Domain joining guides. Removed two sections describing workarounds that no longer apply.
