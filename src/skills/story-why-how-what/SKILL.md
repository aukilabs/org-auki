# Why, How, What

People don't buy what you do — they buy why you do it. Lead with why. Let what follow as proof.

## Structure

- **Why** — your purpose or belief. *"We love music."*
- **How** — the approach that brings the why to life. *"So we want to put music in your pocket."*
- **What** — the product, service, or action. *"The iPod — here are its features and price."*

## Example

**Bad:** *"Today we are introducing the Zune, a music player for $399. We think it will revolutionize how you consume music."*

**Good:** *"We love music. We wish you could bring music with you wherever you go. So we decided we want to put a music player in your pocket. It would need to hold a lot of music, be robust, reliable... so today we're introducing the iPod. 1000 songs in your pocket, 20 minutes of shake memory, several hours of battery — $399."*
