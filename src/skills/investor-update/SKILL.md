# Investor updates

An investor update is memetic propagation. Structure it so investors can easily retell the story at dinner.

## Hook and Ask — most investors will only read this part

- **Hook** — the single most exciting thing that happened. One sentence, punchy, shareable.
- **Ask** — one concrete call to action. Pick one: share this media, introduce us to someone specific, or ask around for something we need. Make it easy to do right now.

## The retell test

If an investor had 30 seconds to tell a friend about Auki at dinner, what would you want them to say? Write that sentence. That's the hook.

---

*Everything below is for investors who keep scrolling.*

## Mission and momentum

Remind them of our mission and strategy. Update on momentum since the last one.

## Traction

Consider live location count or revenue if there's news. What did a customer or partner say this month that every investor should hear?

## Strategy validation

What did we learn that confirms or challenges the perception-first thesis?

## Team

Any exciting staffing changes?
